LD CAPITAL
Company Dossier · The Loan Depot · LD Realty Capital · Est. 1996

LD Capital LLC is a founder-led, Atlanta-based commercial finance group with nearly three decades in hospitality and commercial real estate lending. Per the company's published materials, LD Realty Capital has originated, underwritten, closed, and serviced more than $4.5 billion in long-term fixed-rate commercial real estate loans since 1996 — approaching $5 billion — with deep specialization in hotel finance and the Asian-American hotelier community (AAHOA).

Founder & Leadership

Niraj "Nick" Sheth — Founder, President & CEO. A Toronto native whose father owned and operated hotels, Sheth watched his family struggle to find financing despite strong down payments. After an IT degree and an MBA in Buffalo, he left a six-figure IT career in 1996 to build a "one-stop shop" for hotel financing — starting with $10,000, a fax machine, and a one-bedroom apartment office.

"My business model is to help, protect and educate. I also believe in sharing the wealth. If you do not share your good fortune; if you keep it for yourself, you will lose it."

He credits his wife Meeta Sheth, a pharmacist, with the company's survival: through every downturn she backed the decision to stay in. Long-tenured team members Parag and Asmita Barot stood by the firm through the 2008–2010 crisis, "even when I could not make payroll."

Company History — Three Decades, Three Recessions, Still Standing

1996 — Founded as a hotel-finance brokerage with $10,000 startup capital. First brokered deal: a $5.7M Hampton Inn financing earning $100,000 — reinvested entirely into the firm.
1999 — Relocated to Chattanooga; first AAHOA convention ("We killed it. We broke revenue records."). CMBS becomes the largest revenue line alongside SBA.
2001 — First $1M profit year; formed the LLC. Then 9/11 shut the CMBS market overnight — $1M in prospective profit lost. The pivot: became a bank service provider teaching banks SBA lending. "The only thing we had was SBA, and it saved the day."
2003–2004 — Built a three-building complex in Chattanooga (SBA, CMBS, processing). Launched The Loan Depot Lending Co., Inc. — a non-depository lender for SBA commercial loans. Secured a $75M line of credit.
2007–2008 — Partner split left Sheth with most of the company debt. Acquired four hotels ($30M of assets for $12M) on the eve of the financial crisis — "the worst year ever." Litigation, distressed assets, personal guarantee pressure. His father gave him everything he had; family and friends raised the back payments.
2009–2011 — Rebuilt through receiverships (six bank-assigned properties), asset management, and SBA work. By 2011 the lending arm was back in full swing; a subsequent year produced $320M in hotel loans.
2015 — Relocated to metro Atlanta; restructured as LD Capital LLC — "no more eggs in one basket." Opened an India back office for underwriting and closing, keeping operations running nearly 24 hours a day.
Today — Four-division platform (below); sponsor of the M Helen Hotel development; building the LD Capital digital infrastructure (18 Rust systems, 455 tests) for tokenized real-asset finance.
