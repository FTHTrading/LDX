The Five LDX Standing Rules — Enforced in Every Template Below

  1. No crypto / token / blockchain vocabulary. Say "execution platform," "settlement infrastructure," "institutional safekeeping," "custody arrangement."
  2. No solicitation of investment. The private program is announced only as "by invitation, through official offering documents when released" until the PPM exists.
  3. Only verified claims. $4.5B+ (per LD Realty Capital records), founded 1996, four active divisions, OCC-chartered trust bank custody ARRANGEMENT (never "funds custodied" until the vault is live).
  4. Every outbound piece carries the informational-purposes disclaimer.
  5. Nothing goes out before Niraj Sheth approves final copy.
