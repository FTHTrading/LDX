Dear [First Name],

My name is Niraj Sheth. I founded LD Capital in 1996 with $10,000 and a fax machine, because families like mine — hoteliers whose word was their signature — could not get a fair hearing from the banks. Twenty-nine years later, we have originated, underwritten, closed, and serviced more than $4.5 billion in commercial real estate loans, most of them for the same community. Three recessions, and we are still standing.

I am writing to introduce our firm, and to open a quiet conversation about a program we are preparing for a small group of families.

What we do: long-term fixed-rate commercial real estate origination, SBA and USDA small-business lending, hospitality construction financing, and international investment services (including EB-5). Institutional discipline; relationship-first execution. We service what we originate.

Why I am reaching out now

Over the past year we have completed the largest infrastructure investment in our company's history. We call it LDX — the execution and settlement platform of LD Capital. It brings four things to every transaction we touch, without changing anything about how our clients work with us:

  • Faster closings — draw requests, payoffs, and disbursements measured in days rather than weeks.
  • Institutional-grade safekeeping — client transaction assets safeguarded through custody arrangements with an OCC-chartered national trust bank.
  • Complete transparency — every payment, draw, and document verified, time-stamped, and auditable.
  • The same people, the same standards. Technology serving the relationship — never the other way around.

LDX is the reason I am comfortable opening this conversation. Institutional discipline is what makes it appropriate to speak with a family office at all.

The program we are preparing

LD Capital is preparing a private program through which qualified investors may participate in professionally underwritten commercial real estate debt — the same asset class we have originated for institutions for three decades — administered with registered securities professionals and institutional safekeeping.

Details will be available exclusively through official offering documents when released. I am not soliciting a subscription in this letter, and nothing here constitutes an offer of securities. I am asking whether it would be appropriate for our relationship manager to keep you on a short list of families who are informed when the program opens, on your own terms and timeline.
