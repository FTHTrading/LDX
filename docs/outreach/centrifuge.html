To the Centrifuge team,

LD Capital is a founder-led commercial finance firm based in Atlanta. We have originated, underwritten, closed, and serviced more than $4.5 billion in long-term fixed-rate commercial real estate loans since 1996, with deep specialization in hospitality — including a flagship 90-key SpringHill Suites by Marriott development we sponsor in Helen, Georgia.

We are now completing LDX — our modern execution and settlement platform, sitting underneath the same relationship-first lending business we have run for three decades. We would like to open a conversation with Centrifuge about the institutional Pool infrastructure you have built.

Why the fit is real: Centrifuge Pools were designed for exactly the kind of underwritten, cash-flowing, real-world credit that LD Capital has originated for institutions since 1996. Our current pipeline includes hospitality construction, SBA/USDA-backed small business hospitality, and a rebuilt insurance-recovery restoration book.

What we would explore

  • A pilot Pool tied to the LD Capital origination flow — most likely built around a construction-draw tranche of our SpringHill Suites project (currently under construction; $3.9M spent to date; $27.5M as-complete appraisal).
  • Third-party servicing executed by LD Capital directly — the same discipline we have applied for three decades to institutional CMBS lenders.
  • Compliant investor access through registered securities professionals — Reg D 506(c) for U.S. accredited investors, Reg S for non-U.S. participants.
  • Custody arrangement already executed with an OCC-chartered national trust bank.

What we are asking

A working call with your origination team to discuss what a first LD Capital Pool would need to demonstrate — pool structure, valuation methodology, servicer requirements, and the practical steps between here and a deployed instrument.
