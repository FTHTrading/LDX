LD Capital Announces LDX Execution Platform After $4.5 Billion Lending Milestone

Three-decade hospitality lender modernizes settlement infrastructure with institutional-grade custody arrangement; expanded programs rolling out for hotel developers, small business owners, storm-affected properties, and international investors.

ATLANTA — LD Capital LLC, the hospitality-finance firm founded in 1996 by Niraj “Nick” Sheth, today announced LDX, a modern execution and settlement platform now underpinning its lending operations. The announcement follows the completion of more than $4.5 billion in loans originated, underwritten, closed, and serviced by LD Realty Capital since the firm's founding.

LDX brings institutional-grade transaction safekeeping through an OCC-chartered national trust bank custody arrangement, milestone-verified construction disbursements, and end-to-end transparency to the firm's hospitality, SBA/USDA, and commercial real estate programs.

“After three decades of finding our clients the capital they deserve, we have built the machinery to deliver it with the speed this era demands,” said Sheth. “Same people, same promise — help, protect, and educate.”

Expanded Programs

Alongside the platform announcement, the firm is introducing expanded programs across four fronts:

  • Hospitality Construction-to-Permanent Financing for flagged hotel developers and franchise operators, delivered on LDX execution infrastructure with milestone-verified draws and automated lien-waiver exchange. The firm's flagship SpringHill Suites by Marriott development in Helen, Georgia runs on this platform today.
  • SBA and USDA Lending Paired with Federal Energy Incentive Packaging, coordinating solar investment tax credits, long-runway battery-storage credits, USDA rural energy grants, and accelerated depreciation planning with the client's CPA.
  • Restoration and Insurance-Recovery Lending for owners of storm- and disaster-affected properties, pairing bridge financing with professional claim-documentation support and milestone-gated construction draws.
  • International Investment Services, including LD Capital Bridge to USA — a guided path to U.S. permanent residency through the federal EB-5 program.

Background

LD Capital was founded in 1996 by Niraj Sheth with $10,000 in startup capital and a mission to serve the Asian-American hotelier community and other borrowers underserved by conventional bank credit. The firm's first brokered deal — a $5.7 million Hampton Inn financing — was closed the same year. Over three decades, LD Capital has grown into a four-division commercial finance group: LD Realty Capital (long-term fixed-rate CRE), LD Small Business Finance / The Loan Depot Lending Co. (SBA and USDA lending), LD Residential Mortgage LLC (all 50 states), and LD FinMave (international lending and consulting). The firm maintains a 24-hour global processing operation between its Atlanta headquarters and its India back office.

About LD Capital LLC: Founded 1996, headquartered in Atlanta, Georgia. LD Realty Capital has originated, underwritten, closed, and serviced more than $4.5 billion in commercial real estate loans across three decades and three recessions. Deep specialization in hospitality finance, the AAHOA community, SBA/USDA lending, restoration and insurance recovery, and EB-5 investment immigration.
