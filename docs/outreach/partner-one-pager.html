Who We Are

LD Capital LLC is a founder-led Atlanta-based commercial finance firm. LD Realty Capital has originated, underwritten, closed, and serviced more than $4.5 billion in long-term fixed-rate commercial real estate loans since 1996, with deep specialization in hospitality and the Asian-American hotelier community (AAHOA).

Founded by Niraj “Nick” Sheth with $10,000 and a fax machine, the firm has survived three recessions — 2001, 2008, and every cycle since — through relationship-first underwriting and a mission that has never changed: help, protect, and educate.

What LDX Is

LDX is our modern execution and settlement platform. It sits underneath the lending business our clients already know, and it delivers four things clients feel immediately:

  • Faster closings — draw requests, payoffs, and disbursements measured in days rather than weeks.
  • Institutional-grade safekeeping — client transaction assets safeguarded through custody arrangements with an OCC-chartered national trust bank.
  • Complete transparency — every payment, every draw, every document verified and time-stamped.
  • Same people, same standards — technology serving the relationship, never the other way around.
