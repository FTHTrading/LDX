To the Horizon institutional team,

I lead LD Capital LLC, an Atlanta-based commercial finance firm founded in 1996. Over twenty-nine years, we have originated, underwritten, closed, and serviced more than $4.5 billion in long-term fixed-rate commercial real estate loans — with deep specialization in hospitality finance and the Asian-American hotelier community. We are now completing the LDX execution platform: institutional-grade settlement infrastructure, safekeeping arrangement with an OCC-chartered national trust bank, and milestone-verified construction disbursements.

I am writing to open a conversation about Aave Horizon.

The specific fit: Horizon serves institutional participants with real-world collateral demand. We are a three-decade originator of that collateral. The bridge is registered infrastructure — the Securitize / Chainlink NAVLink route that VBILL uses is a proven path we intend to walk deliberately.

What LD Capital brings to the table

  • Origination franchise. $4.5B+ closed and serviced since 1996 through three recessions. Long-term fixed-rate CRE is our core discipline; we know how these instruments behave in every part of the cycle.
  • Hospitality specialization. Financed hotels from our first deal — a $5.7M Hampton Inn in 1996 — through recent flagged-brand construction. Our sponsor project today is a 90-key SpringHill Suites by Marriott in Helen, Georgia, appraised at $27.5M as-complete.

  • Compliant issuance path. We are aligning with registered broker-dealers and transfer agents so that any institutional-market participation happens under Reg D, Reg A+, or Reg S — never outside the perimeter.

What we are asking

A thirty-minute introductory conversation with your institutional onboarding team. Our preferred outcome is a preliminary framing of what LD Capital-originated CRE tranches — routed through a compliant transfer agent, with Chainlink NAVLink price feeds — would need to demonstrate to be considered for Horizon whitelisting.

We are not asking for capital, a term sheet, or any commitment. We are asking for time and honest guidance from the team that has done this before.
