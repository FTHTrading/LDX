To the Ondo institutional partnerships team,

LD Capital LLC is a 29-year Atlanta-based commercial finance firm — $4.5 billion in commercial real estate loans originated and serviced since 1996, with deep specialization in hospitality and the Asian-American hotelier community. We are now completing the LDX execution platform to modernize how our transactions settle and how our clients access institutional-grade safekeeping.

I would like to open a conversation about points of alignment between our two firms.

Complementarity, not overlap. Ondo has built the leading institutional tokenized-Treasury franchise. LD Capital has spent three decades originating hospitality and CRE debt. Our accredited investor networks — AAHOA principal wealth, EB-5 alumni, family offices we serve — hold both interests: yield-bearing Treasury exposure and real-asset commercial credit.

Where a partnership might live

  • Investor cross-referral. Our qualified U.S. investors seeking short-duration Treasury exposure through OUSG, and our international investors who fit Ondo's non-U.S. products, are a natural audience for your programs — introduced through registered securities professionals.
  • Institutional-grade counterparty. LD Capital's forthcoming Reg D 506(c) private CRE income program will be administered through registered broker-dealers, transfer agents, and OCC-chartered national trust bank safekeeping. Same rigor bar as Ondo's institutional footing.
  • Learning exchange. Ondo's experience navigating the U.S. institutional distribution channel is directly applicable to the LDX rollout. We would value the conversation, whether or not a formal partnership emerges.

What we are asking

A thirty-minute introductory call with your institutional partnerships lead. We are not asking for a co-branded launch or capital commitment — we are asking to introduce LD Capital and understand where your team sees natural alignment.
