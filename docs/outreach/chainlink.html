To the Chainlink institutional team,

I lead LD Capital LLC, an Atlanta-based commercial finance firm with $4.5 billion in commercial real estate loans originated and serviced since 1996. We are 29 years into building a hospitality-CRE origination franchise, and we are now completing LDX — the execution and settlement platform that sits underneath our lending business.

I am reaching out because verified pricing infrastructure is central to what we are building, and Chainlink's NAVLink capability is precisely the piece we need to get right the first time.

Concrete use case: our flagship LD Capital-sponsored project — a 90-key SpringHill Suites by Marriott in Helen, Georgia, appraised at $27.5M as-complete, currently under construction — is being structured for tranched institutional distribution. NAV feeds for that structure need to be verifiable, auditable, and consumable by institutional counterparties on their terms.

What we would explore

  • NAVLink integration for LD Capital-originated tranches. Third-party servicing valuations, appraiser marks, and payment waterfall state — surfaced as verifiable feeds consumable by qualified market participants.
  • Draw-milestone attestation infrastructure. Construction milestone verification (inspector sign-off, lien waivers, contractor pay-app G-703) transformed into verifiable events that trigger disbursement approvals.
  • Cross-institution reference pricing. A path toward the same architecture that already carries Securitize's VBILL into Aave Horizon — applied to LD Capital's hospitality & CRE credit output.

What we are asking

A working conversation with the NAVLink institutional team — thirty to sixty minutes to walk through our origination flow and understand what a first LD Capital deployment would require. We are not asking for anything beyond time and honest engineering guidance.
