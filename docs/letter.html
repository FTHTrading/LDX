To the clients who built this company with us

Twenty-nine years. Three recessions. More than $4.5 billion in loans originated, underwritten, closed, and serviced. And now — our largest step forward yet.

Dear Valued Client,

When we opened our doors in 1996 with ten thousand dollars and a fax machine, we made you one promise: help, protect, and educate. Through the CMBS shutdown after 9/11, through the 2008 crisis that nearly took everything, through every cycle since — that promise is why you are reading this letter, and why we are still here.

Today we are proud to announce the next chapter: LDX — the execution and settlement platform of LD Capital.

Over the past year, we have built and tested a complete modern financing infrastructure — the same caliber of settlement, custody, and compliance technology used by the largest institutions in the country — and placed it underneath the lending business you already know. What that means for you is simple, and none of it requires you to change anything about how you work with us:

Faster closings. Draw requests, payoffs, and disbursements that once took weeks of back-and-forth now move through automated, verified processes measured in days.
Institutional-grade safekeeping. Client transaction assets are safeguarded through custody arrangements with an OCC-chartered national trust bank — the same regulatory standard the nation's largest financial institutions answer to.
Complete transparency. Every payment, every draw, every document — verified, time-stamped, and auditable. You will always know exactly where your transaction stands.
The same people. Same team, same phone number, same standards. The technology serves the relationship — never the other way around.

The “X” in LDX stands for execution — because after three decades of finding our clients the capital they deserve, we have now built the machinery to deliver it with the speed and precision this era demands. This is not a new company, a rebrand, or a departure from what we do. It is the infrastructure our next thirty years will run on.

In the coming weeks we will be introducing expanded programs in hospitality construction financing, SBA and USDA lending paired with federal energy incentives, restoration and insurance-recovery lending, and international investment services. Your relationship manager will reach out personally — or call us any time at 770-272-2232.

Thank you for twenty-nine years of trust. The best is ahead of us.

With gratitude,

Niraj “Nick” Sheth
Founder, President & Chief Executive Officer
