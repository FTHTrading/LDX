I.How to Use This Reference

LD Capital's execution platform uses technology often described elsewhere with speculative vocabulary. We do not use that vocabulary — not because the technology differs, but because precision matters. Every capability we offer has a proper name in banking, securities, and compliance practice.

This lexicon defines each term as we use it, notes the informal word it replaces, and shows the term in a working sentence.

The standing rule: if a term below has a "replaces" line, the replaced word does not appear in LD Capital client materials, marketing, or conversation. When a client uses the informal word, we answer accurately — using ours.

II.The Five LDX Standing Rules

  1. No crypto / token / blockchain vocabulary in client-facing surfaces. Use the institutional term. Every time.
  2. No solicitation of investment. Any private program is described only as "by invitation, through official offering documents when released."
  3. Only verified claims. $4.5B+ (per LD Realty Capital records), founded 1996, four active divisions, OCC-chartered national trust bank custody arrangement executed.
  4. Every outbound piece carries the informational-purposes disclaimer.
  5. Nothing goes out before Niraj Sheth approves final copy.

III.Why This Matters

Institutional finance is a business of trust, and trust is transmitted through language. A client, a regulator, a family office, or a broker-dealer counterparty hearing "wallet" hears one industry. Hearing "custody account" hears another. LD Capital operates in one industry — the regulated one — and our language must reflect that at every touchpoint.

The technology underneath LDX is modern and often shares mechanics with instruments described elsewhere with speculative vocabulary. That is a technical curiosity, not a description of our business. Ownership, settlement, custody, compliance, and capital formation are our business.
